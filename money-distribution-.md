# Money Distribution Rules

## Bronze Pig
- **Price:** 4 TON
- **Distribution:**
    - **Admin Wallet:** 20% (0.8 TON)
    - **Referrer:** 20% (0.8 TON)
    - **Upline (3 Levels):** 60% (2.4 TON) distributed as 20% (0.8 TON) to each of the first 3 levels above the buyer.

## Silver Pig
- **Price:** 40 TON
- **Distribution:**
    - **Admin Wallet:** 20% (8 TON)
    - **Referrer:** 20% (8 TON)
    - **Upline (12 Levels):** 60% (24 TON) distributed as 5% (2 TON) to each of the 12 levels above the buyer.
    - **Requirement:** Only users who hold a Silver Pig are eligible to receive this 5% commission. If a user in the upline holds a Bronze Pig, they will be skipped.

## Gold Pig
- **Price:** 400 TON
- **Distribution:**
    - **Admin Wallet:** 20% (80 TON)
    - **Referrer:** 20% (80 TON)
    - **Upline (12 Levels):** 60% (240 TON) distributed as 5% (20 TON) to each of the 12 levels above the buyer.
    - **Requirement:** Only users who hold a Gold Pig are eligible to receive this 5% commission.

## Diamond Pig
- **Price:** 4000 TON
- **Distribution:**
    - **Admin Wallet:** 20% (800 TON)
    - **Referrer:** 20% (800 TON)
    - **Upline (12 Levels):** 60% (2400 TON) distributed as 5% (200 TON) to each of the 12 levels above the buyer.
    - **Requirement:** Only users who hold a Diamond Pig are eligible to receive this 5% commission.

---
> NOTE: It's obvious that in each level if a user reward receivers are not eligible to receive the rewards any left reward will be transferred to the developers wallet.
